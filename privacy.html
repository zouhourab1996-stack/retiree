Privacy Policy

The short version: your retirement date never leaves your device. We run no accounts, no newsletters, no forms.

Date storage: the countdown date you enter is saved in your browser's localStorage, on your device only. Clearing your browser data removes it. It is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Analytics: we use Google Analytics to count page views in aggregate (via cookies). You can block this with any standard browser setting or extension.

Advertising: when ads appear, they are served by Google AdSense, which uses cookies to show relevant ads. See Google's Ads & Your Data policies for details and opt-outs.

Contact: this site is operated by the retiree.bond project team.

What a page view records

A request for a page tells our host which page was asked for, roughly when, and the country it came from. That is the minimum any website needs in order to function. There are no accounts and no forms, and nothing you type into a calculator is transmitted anywhere — the calculators run on your device and discard the input when you close the tab.

Advertising and analytics

We may load third-party analytics and advertising scripts. Those third parties set their own cookies and build their own profiles under their own policies, which is how interest-based advertising works across the web generally. None of them see anything you type into a calculator on this site, because nothing you type leaves your device.

You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google's ad settings, or industry-wide through the Network Advertising Initiative and the Digital Advertising Alliance. Blocking third-party cookies achieves much the same, and every page here works without them.

Your rights

Because we hold no accounts and collect no personal information, there is normally nothing for us to produce, correct or delete — and we will say so plainly rather than going quiet. If you are in a jurisdiction with access or erasure rights and want them exercised, reach us through the contact route in the footer.

Children

The site is general-audience and not directed at children. Since nothing is collected in the first place, there is nothing to collect from a child either.
