Retirement Notice Period Checklist

The date you give notice is not always the date your employment, pay, benefits or access ends. Put each milestone on its own line before you announce retirement.

Quick date map

MilestoneWhat to confirm
Notice dateWhen the employer receives your written notice.
Final working dayThe last day you are expected to work or hand over duties.
Employment end dateThe date HR records as the end of employment.
Final payPay date, unused leave, bonus rules and deductions.
Benefits end dateHealth, life, pension and other coverage dates.

What to include in the handover

  • Open projects, deadlines and named owners.
  • Recurring tasks, supplier contacts and approved procedures.
  • Location of files without sharing confidential personal data.
  • Equipment return, access closure and a final confirmation email.

Before choosing a countdown date

Use our retirement date versus last day guide and check the separate healthcare gap checklist. A countdown is a planning aid, not an employment or benefits determination.

FAQ

How much notice should I give before retiring?

It depends on your contract, workplace policy and local rules. Check the written policy and ask HR before relying on a generic number.

Is my last day the same as my retirement date?

Not necessarily. Pension, employment, benefits and final-pay dates can be different.
