Working Days in 2027: 249 on the Federal Calendar

2027 has 261 weekdays and 249 working days on the U.S. federal calendar. This month-by-month guide explains the observed holidays, the December 31 surprise, and how to turn the total into a retirement countdown.

Working Days in 2027: 249 on the Federal Calendar

2027 has 261 weekdays. Take out the 12 weekdays that federal employees get off and you are left with 249 working days. The number is 12 rather than the usual 11 because New Year's Day 2028 falls on a Saturday, so the federal observance moves back to Friday, December 31, 2027. That one date is the reason many 2027 working-day counts online are off by one. If you are counting down to a retirement date, the difference matters.

Federal calendar, bank calendar, your calendar

These counts assume a Monday-to-Friday job that follows the federal schedule. Your employer may not. One source notes that Federal Reserve Banks stay open on December 31, 2027 even though federal offices close, and private employers set their own lists. If your workplace skips a holiday on this table, add that day back. If it gives extra days, subtract them. The method stays the same.

Three counting mistakes to avoid

  • Counting weekdays and stopping there. 261 weekdays is not 261 working days.
  • Using the printed date, not the observed one. Juneteenth, July 4 and Christmas all fall on weekends in 2027. The day off moves, and the working-day count follows the day off.
  • Forgetting the year-end spillover. December 31, 2027 belongs to 2028's holiday list but removes a day from 2027's count.

Where the 2027 calendar helps you

The end of the year is the best leverage. Christmas is observed on Friday, December 24 and New Year's Day 2028 on Friday, December 31. Take leave from Monday, December 27 through Thursday, December 30, four days of leave, and you are off from December 24 through January 2, 2028, which is ten days in a row. A similar quiet gift comes in the summer: Juneteenth on Friday, June 18 makes a natural three-day weekend, and Independence Day gives another with Monday, July 5.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many working days are in 2027?
On the federal holiday calendar, 249: 261 weekdays minus 12 observed holidays. If you only subtract the 11 holidays listed in the statute, you get 250, which misses the December 31 observance.

Is December 31, 2027 a federal holiday?
For most federal employees, yes. New Year's Day 2028 falls on a Saturday, and the observed holiday moves back to Friday, December 31, 2027. Some institutions, such as Federal Reserve Banks, handle it differently.

When are Juneteenth and Independence Day observed in 2027?
Juneteenth falls on Saturday, June 19 and is observed Friday, June 18. Independence Day falls on Sunday, July 4 and is observed Monday, July 5.

How do I count working days to my retirement date?
List every Monday-to-Friday date between today and the date, then remove your employer's holidays using the observed day off. Counting today or not is a choice. Pick one and stay consistent.

249 is a useful number, and your own count to a retirement date is usually more motivating than a full-year total. Pick the date, count the days that are actually working days, and watch the number fall.

What the working-day count leaves out

The figure on this page strips weekends and the leave you would realistically take. It does not strip public holidays, because those vary by country, by state and by employer. In the United States there are eleven federal holidays, and most employers observe around ten — but a shift worker, a teacher and a contractor will each have a different answer, so we leave the subtraction to you rather than guessing.

Two other things are not in the number. Sick leave is not, because it is unpredictable. And unpaid leave or a career break is not, because only you know whether one is coming. If you are planning against the figure rather than just enjoying it, subtract those from the total and work from there.

Why the working-day figure is worth more than the calendar one

A 4,000-day countdown sounds like a very long time. Roughly 2,860 of those days are weekdays, and once leave and holidays come off, the number you are actually working is smaller still. That is not meant to be grim — it is the number that makes planning possible, because it is the one measured in units you control.
