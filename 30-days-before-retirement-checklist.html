30 Days Before Retirement: A Final-Month Checklist

The last month before retirement is easier when the important dates and handover details are written down. Use this checklist as an administrative prompt, then confirm every plan-specific detail with your employer, pension administrator and healthcare provider.

This is a general checklist. Employment rules, benefit dates, pension processing and healthcare arrangements vary by country, employer and plan.

Week 4: Confirm the dates and paperwork

  • Confirm your last working day, employment end date and retirement effective date in writing.
  • Check whether unused leave, final salary, bonus or other payments are handled before or after your final day.
  • Ask HR or your plan administrator for the status of any pension or benefit application and whether any documents are still missing.
  • Confirm when health coverage ends and when any replacement coverage begins; do not assume the dates match your last day at work.
  • Save copies of benefit statements, plan letters, employment records and contact details in a secure personal location.

Week 3: Prepare the handover

  • Agree with your manager on the work that must be completed, transferred or documented.
  • Write down recurring tasks, project status, key contacts and where approved work files are stored.
  • Return company property according to policy, including devices, keys, cards and equipment.
  • Ask how access to work systems will be closed and how to retrieve any personal records you are entitled to keep.
  • Do not copy confidential employer, customer or colleague data to personal accounts or devices.

Week 2: Check payroll, benefits and practical arrangements

  • Verify the expected final pay date and the contact for payroll questions.
  • Check the treatment of remaining leave and any outstanding expenses with payroll or HR.
  • Confirm the first expected pension or benefit payment date with the administrator; processing dates can differ from eligibility dates.
  • Review direct-deposit details through the official administrator channel, not through links in unexpected messages.
  • Make a list of any appointments, renewals or administrative tasks that need attention after leaving work.

Week 1: Make the final days manageable

  • Complete the agreed handover and send a concise status note to the relevant colleagues.
  • Check that you have personal copies of permitted documents and contact information.
  • Return remaining equipment and obtain confirmation if your employer provides it.
  • Confirm the time and process for your final day, including any exit meeting or access-card return.
  • Thank colleagues and set expectations about how you would like to stay in touch.

On your final working day

Keep the day simple. Complete the agreed handover, return property, confirm that required forms have been received, and keep the name and contact details of the person who can answer follow-up questions. If a pension or benefit payment has not started, contact the official administrator rather than assuming it will begin automatically.

Three dates worth keeping separate

DateWhat it means
Last working dayThe final day you are expected to work or attend your workplace.
Employment end dateThe date your employment formally ends; it may differ from your last day on site.
Pension or benefit start dateThe date a plan or administrator says payments or coverage begin; confirm it directly.

FAQ

What if my pension application is still processing?

Contact the official plan administrator, ask whether anything is outstanding, and request the current expected timeline in writing. Do not assume the payment date is the same as your retirement date.

Should I tell colleagues my personal plans?

That is your choice. Share only what you are comfortable sharing and follow your employer's normal communication and confidentiality policies.

Can this checklist tell me whether I am eligible to retire?

No. Eligibility depends on your specific pension plan, employer, country and circumstances. Confirm it with the relevant administrator.
