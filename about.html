About retiree.bond

A free retirement countdown: days, work days and paychecks until the date you choose. No account, no email, no cost.

retiree.bond was built on a simple observation: people approaching retirement count. Daily. They count calendar days, they count work days, they count paychecks — and the tools for it were either spreadsheets or nothing. So we built the counter we kept hearing described.

Your retirement date is stored only in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to any server, and there is no account to leak. The site is supported by advertising when ads appear.

We show official eligibility ages (Social Security full retirement age, Medicare at 65) and count days. We do not give financial, tax, or medical advice — for decisions that shape decades, see a qualified fee-only advisor.

What the calculators actually do

The countdown tools work from two inputs — a date of birth and a target retirement age — and convert the gap into the units people plan with: calendar days, working days, weekends, paychecks and hours. The working-day figure is the one that surprises people most, because it strips out weekends and the leave you would realistically take, which turns a number that sounds enormous into one you can actually picture.

Everything runs in your browser. No date of birth is uploaded, nothing is stored, and closing the tab discards it. There is no email gate, because a countdown is not worth your inbox.

Where the official numbers come from

The age thresholds used across the site follow the Social Security Administration's schedule: full retirement age is 67 for anyone born in 1960 or later, early claiming is possible from 62 with a permanent reduction, and delayed retirement credits accrue up to age 70. Medicare eligibility begins at 65 regardless of when you claim retirement benefits — the two are separate decisions and conflating them is one of the more expensive mistakes people make.

Those are federal rules. Anything specific to your own record — your earnings history, your pension, your state's treatment of the income — depends on details we do not have and cannot guess at.

What this site is not

It is not financial advice, and nothing here tells you when you should claim. The calculators answer how long, not what to do. For a decision that large, talk to someone who can see your whole picture — the numbers on this site are the ones that help you ask better questions, not the ones that answer them.
