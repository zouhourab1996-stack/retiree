90 Days Before Retirement: A Practical Checklist

The final 90 days are less about guessing an age and more about confirming dates. Use this checklist to turn a retirement countdown into a short list of questions for your employer, plan administrator and household.

At a glance

  • Confirm the last day of employment in writing.
  • Ask when health coverage, payroll and employer contributions end.
  • List remaining paychecks, leave and benefit elections.
  • Prepare a handover without sharing private data.
  • Keep official statements and deadlines in one folder.

90 to 61 days: confirm the rules

Ask HR or your plan administrator for the written retirement, pension and healthcare rules that apply to you. Confirm whether your last working day must be before a birthday, month-end or payroll cutoff. A general retirement calculator cannot decide your eligibility.

60 to 31 days: check the money flow

Use the paycheck countdown to estimate how many regular paydays remain, then compare it with payroll’s official schedule. Ask how unused leave is paid, when the final paycheck arrives and which deductions continue. For tax, investment or benefits choices, use a qualified professional.

30 to 8 days: protect the handover

Write a simple list of recurring tasks, owners, open decisions and key dates. Remove passwords and personal information from the handover. Return equipment according to your employer’s process and save copies of documents you are allowed to keep.

FAQ

Is 90 days enough to prepare for retirement?

It can be enough for an administrative checklist, but not necessarily for every financial or benefits decision. Start earlier whenever possible.

Can a countdown tell me when I can retire?

No. It counts down to a date you choose. Eligibility depends on your plan, employer, country and personal circumstances.
