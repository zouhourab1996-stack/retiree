The three universal rules

1. End of the month. Pensions and annuities start on the first of the following month — almost everywhere.
2. End of the pay period. Complete the period, keep the full accrual — private and public sector alike.
3. End of the year. Bonuses, leave payouts and matches often vest or pay at year end.

Teachers

The June wave is real — Georgia TRS documents the "large volume of retirements at the end of each school year" (trsga.com; June 15 rule per trs.texas.gov). Deadlines (GA TRS): June 1 retirement → apply by March 31 · July 1 → April 30 · August 1 → May 31. Texas adds the June 15 rule: retire effective May 31, work through June 15.

Private sector

Three checks before you pick: (1) finish the pay period, (2) confirm your 401(k) match is vested — many plans vest on year-end or anniversary dates, (3) the last day of the month keeps benefits continuous. Last Friday of a month, late in the year, ticks every box for most people.

Questions people ask

What is the best month to retire?

It depends on your sector: federal employees do best at the end of the year (lump sum + January annuity), teachers at the end of a school year (June), and private-sector employees at the end of a pay period and month — often the last Friday of December.

What are the best FERS retirement dates in 2027?

Per Federal News Network: January 9, April 30, May 29, October 30 and December 31 — pay-period ends aligned with month boundaries.

Why do teachers retire in June or July?

Because contracts end with the school year. Georgia TRS alone sees a 'large volume of retirements at the end of each school year' — and Texas teachers can retire effective May 31 while working through June 15.

Is retiring at the end of the year really better?

For federal employees, usually yes: about 40% of federal retirements happen in the year-end window, combining the holiday period, the annual-leave lump sum and a clean January annuity start.

What's the best day of the week to retire?

Friday — it caps the week, closes a pay period cleanly, and turns your first Monday into the first day of the rest of your life.
